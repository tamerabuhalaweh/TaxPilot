What this package is testing

  • Whether the navy and jade workspace feels credible for an accountant without becoming decorative.
  • Whether the evidence-first hierarchy makes a difference, a duplicate, and an inconclusive currency readable at a glance.
  • Whether the same information survives at 390px and in Arabic without hiding financial truth.
  • Whether the proposed direction is worth implementing without rewriting the working review flow.

What it does not claim

  • No live application, backend dashboard endpoint, or persisted review-state workflow is represented.
  • No production data, customer records, tax advice, approvals, or risk scores are shown.
  • Arabic covers the reconciliation workspace only; PDF and XLSX exports remain English-only.
  • Implementation waits for explicit founder and GPT-6 acceptance.
